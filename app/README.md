# Dated Base44 app snapshots (historical)

> **Historical snapshots.** `base44-lock-2026-10-12/` and `base44-lock-2026-10-13/` are dated copies of the Base44 lock app as it was on those days. They show the Revision 6 Handshake rules of that time (2.50% Handshake fee, $0.95 to $1.05 Handshake band, a Handshake identity and allowance count in the proof tuple). Those rules are superseded by Revision 12 (`docs/Vinculum_Finalis_Master_Specification_Revision_12_2026-10-16.md`): 5% fee, $0.01 to $0.02 band, 1 hour, no allowance limit, and no handshakeIdentity or allowance counter in the lock contract.

These folders are kept for the record and for ABI comparison tests. They are not the live app and are not edited.
